About the Project

Experience-Driven AI Safety

A Sem 6 AI project — shifting women's safety from emergency response to preventive awareness using NLP.

🧠

NLP-Powered Analysis

TF-IDF vectorization with 1–3 gram range and 15,000 max features feeds a calibrated LinearSVC model for high-accuracy sentiment classification across Negative / Neutral / Positive labels.

😨

Emotion Detection

Keyword-based emotion classifier detects Fear, Anxiety, and Comfort from experience narratives — matching the model's fear_words, anxiety_words, and comfort_words dictionaries exactly.

🛡️

Preventive Awareness

Unlike SOS apps, the system identifies unsafe patterns before incidents occur — enabling proactive civic planning and policy-level insights from aggregated community data.

🔒

Ethically Designed

No real-time GPS tracking. Fully anonymous submissions. No personal identifiers. Focuses purely on community awareness, not surveillance.
